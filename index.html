How I work

I take products from idea to something real people use — the app itself, the backend behind it, and the unglamorous parts (privacy policies, store compliance, push notifications) that actually let it ship.

Most recently that's meant native builds on both iOS and Android sharing one backend, and engines with genuine logic underneath — a puzzle solver, a balance-tested combat system — rather than just interface work.
